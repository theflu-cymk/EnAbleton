**All sales are final. No refunds are offered.**

Because the product is delivered immediately as source and installable files,
there is nothing to return. Please read [LIMITATIONS.md](LIMITATIONS.md) and the

are the only supported combination today, and the audio-analysis and automated
edit features report uncertainty rather than guaranteeing a musical result.

If a purchase was charged twice, or the download cannot be delivered at all,
that is a billing or delivery failure rather than a refund request, and it will

## Support

- **Channel:** open an issue at
  <https://github.com/theflu-cymk/EnAbleton/issues>. Issue access is granted to
  licence holders.
- **Response time:** within five business days.
- **Scope:** installation, connection to Live, and behaviour of the documented
  commands. Musical judgement, third-party plug-ins, Ableton licensing and
  operating-system problems are outside support scope.
- **Before reporting:** run `enableton setup` and `enableton doctor`, and include
  the JSON they print with secrets removed. They report the installed version,
  the running bridge version and the loopback port.

A dedicated support email address is to be added to the purchase page and this
file before public sale; until then the issue tracker is the support channel.
